About CalmDose

Designed around real everyday life so caring for your health—or helping someone you love—feels calm and effortless.

The Problem

Millions of people miss or mistime their medicines, and confusing health tools make everyday care harder than it should be.

Parents managing a child's short-term syrup, working adults rushing through busy mornings, and older adults balancing several daily pills all run into the same hurdle: doses get forgotten or taken late because paper lists get lost and most apps are crowded with tiny text and complex setups. When medicines are missed or mistimed, recovery slows down, hospital visits become more likely, and families carry unnecessary worry every single day.

Easy to forget

Busy schedules and shifting meal times make a single morning or evening dose easy to overlook.

Hard to keep track

Sticky notes and separate phone alarms leave caregivers unsure whether today's pill was already taken.

No simple tool for all ages

Crowded screens, mandatory logins, and medical jargon push older users and teens away from daily tracking.

Our Solution: CalmDose turns daily medicine routines into one large, friendly checklist right in your browser—ready in seconds with zero accounts.

Our Mission

We believe caring for your daily health should feel reassuring, calm, and clear at every age. Our mission is to give individuals and families an accessible, private space to follow daily medicine routines without confusion.

Our Story

CalmDose began after seeing how hard it can be to juggle paper pill charts and complicated phone apps that require accounts just to log a morning vitamin. Kenedy Duwarah built this single, readable page with large buttons, plain words, and complete privacy—so anyone from a teenager to a grandparent can use it comfortably on day one.

Three Gentle Health Tips

Pair pills with an existing habit

Linking a morning capsule to brushing your teeth or making tea makes routines easier to remember naturally.

Keep medicines in a cool, dry spot

Bathroom cabinets can get steamy. A dry bedroom drawer or kitchen shelf away from sunlight helps protect tablets.

Ask before splitting or skipping

If a pill feels hard to swallow or causes discomfort, ask your pharmacist or doctor before changing the dose.
